## Smart Public Toilet for Ahmedabad 🚻

### The Problem

Public toilets are important in a city, but finding a clean, accessible and well-maintained toilet can still be difficult in busy areas.

So we thought:

**What if a public toilet could be designed to be cleaner, more sustainable and smarter from the beginning?**

### Our Idea

We designed a **smart public toilet concept for Ahmedabad** with a focus on cleanliness, accessibility and sustainability.

The design includes:

- ☀️ Solar power
- 🌱 Green rooftop
- ♿ Accessibility features
- 🚻 Modern and hygienic facilities
- 📍 Easy location/finding concept
- 💡 Smart monitoring for maintenance and occupancy

### Why Ahmedabad?

Ahmedabad is a rapidly growing city with many crowded public places.

Our idea is to place these smart toilets in areas where people need them the most, making public sanitation more convenient for everyone.

### Gemini + Nano Banana 🤖

We used **Google Gemini** to brainstorm the problem and develop the solution.

We used **Nano Banana** to turn our idea into a visual concept and show what the final public toilet could look like.

### Our Goal 🎯

To create a public toilet that is:

**Clean • Accessible • Sustainable • Smart**
